# Project Instructions

## Privacy and Notion

- Treat the published travel book and every Git-tracked file as potentially public.
- Never copy overly private or identifying information into the website, source code, static assets, commit messages, screenshots, or test output. This includes personal names, booking or reservation references, ticket numbers, verification codes, voucher or QR contents, passport details, private phone numbers or email addresses, payment information, and account identifiers.
- When updating the site from Notion, copy only share-safe itinerary facts such as dates, times, routes, public venues, public business contact details, and general travel notes.
- Keep private trip details in Notion. On the website, use a neutral note such as `私人資訊請至 Notion 查看` instead of reproducing those details.
- Do not expose private Notion page or database URLs on the published website unless the user explicitly requests it.
- If it is unclear whether a field is safe to publish, omit it and keep it in Notion.
- Before committing or publishing, inspect the changed files for personal identifiers, booking codes, ticket data, vouchers, and QR content.
